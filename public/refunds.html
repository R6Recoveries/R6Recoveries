Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

Refunds

Refunds are considered case-by-case before substantial work begins; once work has begun, refunds generally are not available.

Before Work Begins

If you request a refund before substantial work has begun, R6 Recoveries may review the request based on the circumstances of the order, its current status, and any work already performed.

After Work Begins

Once substantial work has begun, refunds generally are not available because time and service work have already been committed to the order.

How to Request a Review

Contact R6 Recoveries with your order number and a clear explanation of the request. Refund decisions are handled individually.
