COMMON QUESTIONS.

What information is requested during order intake?

We collect detailed telemetry regarding your monitor's refresh rate, GPU/CPU specs, primary operational issues (e.g., stuttering, input lag), and current Siege sensitivity.

Is an account required to launch an operation?

Yes. Accounts power secure order tracking, private status updates, and custom technical reports inside your dashboard.

Can I test payment flows locally?

Yes. Demo payment mode activates automatically when Stripe API keys are not configured in your environment.

Are login credentials stored or requested?

No. We never ask for or store passwords for Rainbow Six, Ubisoft Connect, Discord, or Steam accounts. All passwords use bcrypt hashing.
