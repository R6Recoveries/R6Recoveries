Privacy at R6 Recoveries

R6 Recoveries collects information needed to create customer accounts, process recovery orders, communicate about those orders, provide the requested service, and maintain the security of the service.

Information You Provide

This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your R6 username
  • Your Discord username
  • Platform information
  • Recovery details you provide
  • Information you choose to include with your order

Payments

Payments are handled through the configured payment provider. R6 Recoveries does not ask customers to submit payment-card information through the recovery intake form.

How Information Is Used

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process and track orders.
  • To communicate about your order and provide support.
  • To review, secure, maintain, and improve the service.

Information We Do Not Want

Do not submit passwords, payment-card information, Steam credentials, Ubisoft credentials, or Discord authentication tokens through the recovery intake.

Contact
